How it works

Three keys. No lost links.

01⌘C

Copy a link

From Safari, Slack, X or anywhere else. It never leaves your Mac.

02⌥S

Stash it

The popover opens with the link ready. Press Return and trackers are stripped.

03⌘E

Find it later

Search from the menu bar, or open the canvas to browse every link as a card.

Private by default.

Your clipboard is read on your Mac, only to spot links. 46 kinds of tracking parameters are removed before anything is saved.

No account needed.

Start as a guest. Sign in with Apple or Google later and every link syncs across your Macs.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Stashbar is free and independent, so it isn’t sold through the App Store. macOS asks you to confirm it once on first launch.

1Download and dragOpen Stashbar.dmg and drag Stashbar into Applications.
2Open it onceOpen Stashbar from Applications. If macOS says it can’t verify the developer, click Done.
3Click “Open Anyway”System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Download, drag to Applications, done.

Do you read my clipboard?

Only to spot links, and only on your Mac.

What if I delete the app?

Guests lose their links. Signed-in users get them all back.

Which Macs does it support?

macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

How do I quit it completely?

Right-click the pin in the menu bar and choose Quit Stashbar. It stops fully — nothing keeps running in the background.
