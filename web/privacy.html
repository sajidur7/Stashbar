Your links are yours.

The short version: we don’t sell data, we don’t read your clipboard off your Mac, and guests never touch our servers.

01

What we store

When signed in: the links you stash (URL, title, tags, notes, pinned/archived state), your email and name from Apple or Google, an optional profile photo, and the names of Macs linked to your account. Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and only your account can read it. As a guest, nothing leaves your Mac.

02

Your clipboard

Stashbar checks the clipboard for web addresses on your Mac only. Nothing you copy is uploaded unless you choose to stash it. Items marked as concealed by password managers are ignored.

03

Sign in

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication with Apple or Google. We never see your password. When you stash a link, Stashbar may fetch that page once to read its title and preview image.

04

Analytics

Stashbar has no analytics or tracking. We don’t collect usage counts, crash reports or advertising IDs.

05

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete account removes your links, devices, profile and sign-in from our servers immediately. Guests can delete their data by deleting the app.

06

Contact
